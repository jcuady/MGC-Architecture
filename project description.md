06 Architectural Capstone
Project Type: Institutional
Date: 2025
Status: Academic Works
Role: Independent Designer - Collaboration
Introduction
Longos Central Elementary School was reimagined as a learning environment that better
supports both students and teachers. The proposal focuses on creating classrooms and
shared spaces that are more comfortable, functional, and ready to meet the needs of future
generations.
Design Problem
A closer look at the existing campus revealed overcrowded classrooms, poor ventilation,
excessive heat, limited daylight, and aging facilities that affect everyday learning. These
challenges became the foundation for a design that puts student comfort, well-being, and
learning first.
Design Approach
With Needs, Design, and Comfort as the guiding principles, every space was planned to be
flexible, inclusive, and easy to navigate. The campus layout also strengthens connections
between buildings, introduces more green spaces, and incorporates vernacular-inspired
elements to give the school a stronger sense of identity.
Design Strategy
One of the key priorities was improving thermal comfort inside the classrooms. Through
solar analysis, the placement and density of sun-shading panels were carefully adjusted
based on each building's orientation, helping reduce heat and glare while bringing in
comfortable natural daylight.

07 Saro
Project Type: Institutional
Date: 2023
Status: Academic Works
Role: Independent Designer
Description:
Saro is a conceptual pavilion inspired by the traditional Tausug salakot, celebrating both its
practical purpose and its cultural significance in Filipino heritage. The design reinterprets
the familiar form of the salakot into a contemporary architectural structure that offers
shade, shelter, and a welcoming gathering space. Beyond its functional role, the pavilion
serves as a tribute to Filipino identity by blending traditional inspiration with modern
design thinking. The project highlights how cultural references can be thoughtfully
translated into meaningful architectural experiences.
