● Visit and assess the site
● Discuss your needs, budget, and priorities

● Share your vision and requirements

04 — Permits & Contracts
What We’ll Do
● Prepare permit documents
● Assist with permit processing
● Prepare the construction contract
● Coordinate with the required offices
What You’ll Do
● Submit required property documents
● Sign necessary forms and agreements
● Settle applicable government fees
You’ll Receive
● Permit-ready documents
● Signed construction agreement
● Approved permits (once released)
Estimated Time
1–2 months

What We’ll Do
● Begin construction
● Monitor project progress
● Coordinate with the construction team
● Address site concerns
● Keep you updated throughout the project
What You’ll Do
● Approve major decisions when needed
● Join scheduled site visits (optional)
● Release scheduled payments
You’ll Receive
● Regular progress updates
● Site reports
● Completed project
Estimated Time
6–12 months (depending on the project size and complexity)

Finish Level - Cost Estimate Calculator
Bare Finish: A basic bare home with concrete flooring, unpainted concrete walls, minimal
windows, and an exposed ceiling.
Standard Finish: A clean and practical home with tiled flooring, painted walls, standard
aluminum-framed windows, and a simple flat ceiling.
Premium Finish: A refined home with large-format tiles or engineered wood flooring,
decorative wall cladding, full-height glass windows, and detailed ceilings.
Luxury Finish: A premium customized home with natural stone or solid wood flooring,
imported wall finishes, double-glazed windows, and custom wood or acoustic ceilings.
